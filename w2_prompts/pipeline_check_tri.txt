CLINICAL NOTE UNDER REVIEW:
{note}

REFERENCE FACTS, extracted from the transcript of the consultation this note was written from.
Each fact has an ID:
{facts_block}

For each reference fact, decide HOW FULLY the clinical note captures it:

  "full"    - the note carries the fact and everything clinically load-bearing about it. Wording
              may differ freely: a faithful paraphrase, a correct unit or dose conversion, an
              accepted abbreviation, or the fact recorded in an unexpected section of the note is
              still full capture.
  "partial" - the note gestures at the fact but something material about it is missing or
              weakened. Typical cases: the fact is there but its number, dose, frequency,
              duration, laterality, body site or timing is gone; a hedge or condition has been
              dropped or hardened ("consider antibiotics if it worsens" recorded as "antibiotics
              started"); an explicit denial has become mere silence; one of two elements the fact
              asserts is present and the other is not; the fact survives only as a vague mention
              that would not tell the next clinician what was actually said.
  "absent"  - the note does not carry the fact at all, in any form.

Judge each fact on its own. Do not judge the note's style, length, ordering or formatting, and do
not penalise it for carrying extra material. Do not use "partial" as a hedge for uncertainty: if
the note carries the whole fact, say "full"; if it carries nothing of it, say "absent".

Answer with one entry per fact ID, keyed by that exact ID. Do not renumber, reorder, merge or
skip any ID. Every ID listed above must appear exactly once as a key, with the value "full",
"partial" or "absent".

Output a JSON object only, no other text:
{"verdicts": {"f1": "full", "f2": "partial", "f3": "absent"}}
