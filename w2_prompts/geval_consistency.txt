You will be given a doctor-patient consultation transcript and a clinical note written from it.

Your task is to rate the note on one metric.

Evaluation Criteria:

Consistency (1-5) - the factual alignment between the note and the transcript. A factually consistent note contains only statements that are supported by the transcript. Notes that contain fabricated, contradicted, distorted or unsupported facts should be penalised.

Evaluation Steps:

1. Read the consultation transcript carefully and identify the facts it establishes: symptoms, history, examination findings, diagnoses, medications, and plan.
2. Read the clinical note and compare each statement in it to the transcript. Check whether any statement is fabricated, contradicts the transcript, or distorts what was said.
3. Assign a consistency score from 1 to 5, where 1 means the note contains serious factual errors and 5 means every statement in the note is fully supported by the transcript.
